## Rationale


The root asks how blockchain-based markets can coordinate South Africa's economy. Before any case
is chosen, the MSc needs to know what has already been studied in South Africa: which sectors, which
methods, and with what outcomes. Then the contribution can be placed against that work and not
claimed in a vacuum. This review also tells 2f which aspects already have evidence and which are
open.

What each candidate conclusion means here:

- **converged**: the verified SA literature (peer-reviewed work, theses, and central-bank research
  such as SARB's Project Khokha) gives one consistent picture. It shows where blockchain has been
  applied or studied in South Africa, and its outcomes do not conflict across studies. That picture
  is adopted as the related-work baseline. Even if no study addresses market coordination, this
  candidate still applies, provided the literature agrees.
- **contested**: studies of the same SA sector or mechanism reach conflicting outcomes, or the
  literature is too fragmented to summarise as one picture. The Finding names the competing
  accounts and states which baseline is adopted and why.
