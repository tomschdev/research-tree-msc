## Rationale

The root targets the highest-impact setting, not the whole economy. Building on 2d, this review
identifies which aspect of South Africa's economy has coordination failures that a blockchain-based
market could plausibly address, and justifies the choice. The cases will be drawn from that aspect.

What each candidate conclusion means here:

- **converged**: the literature points clearly to one aspect (or one tightly related cluster) as
  the highest-impact site of coordination failure; it is adopted as the focus.
- **contested**: several aspects compete (e.g. labour-market matching versus coordination in the
  informal economy); the Finding states which is chosen, on which criteria, and why.
