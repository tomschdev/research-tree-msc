## Rationale

This review joins 2a and 2b. It defines a blockchain-based market and collects the emergent
properties claimed for it (coordination among strangers, programmable incentives, lower verification
and networking costs), along with the theoretical and empirical evidence for each. It is where the
root's premise, that blockchain-based markets coordinate, gets its support or loses it.

What each candidate conclusion means here:

- **converged**: the literature agrees on what a blockchain-based market is and which properties it
  has; these are adopted, with the strength of evidence for each property recorded.
- **contested**: accounts differ on the definition, or on whether the claimed properties hold; the
  Finding states which account is adopted, which properties are well supported, and which are not.
