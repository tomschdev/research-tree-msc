## Rationale

The root asks whether blockchain-based markets coordinate better than the alternatives. That needs an
account of how markets work as a coordination mechanism: how prices, matching and contracts align
the behaviour of dispersed participants; what transaction and information costs they carry; and when
markets fail to coordinate. This review supplies the vocabulary and the measures of coordination the
case questions will use.

What each candidate conclusion means here:

- **converged**: the literature offers a shared account of market mechanics and of coordination; it
  is adopted as the working basis, with sources.
- **contested**: rival accounts (e.g. price-based, transaction-cost, market-design) define or measure
  coordination differently; the Finding states which is adopted and why.
