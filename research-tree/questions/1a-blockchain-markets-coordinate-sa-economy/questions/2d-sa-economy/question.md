## Rationale

The root applies the mechanism to South Africa. This review describes the economy the mechanism has
to operate in: dual or fragmented structure, unemployment, the informal sector, information asymmetry
between employers and job-seekers, and the effects of language, culture and history. It supplies the
context against which 2e's choice of focus is made.

What each candidate conclusion means here:

- **converged**: the literature agrees on the economy's defining characteristics; they are adopted
  as the working description, with sources.
- **contested**: accounts differ (e.g. structural or demand-side explanations versus explanations
  based on coordination or matching failure); the Finding states which characterisation is adopted
  and why.
